The Impact of Diesel Prices on Trucking Fuel Costs

VUB - Advanced Mathematics Project

2026-2027

Research Question

How do changes in diesel prices affect trucking fuel costs in the United States?

Data Source

Monthly US diesel prices from 2020 to 2025, covering 72 observations.

Source: U.S. Energy Information Administration via FRED.

Original Dataset

Analysis 1: Descriptive Statistics

Average diesel price: $3.745 per gallon

Maximum price: $5.754

Minimum price: $2.389

Standard deviation: $0.822

View Graph 1 (PDF)

Analysis 2: Fuel Cost Estimation

Assumptions: 10,000 miles per month and 6 miles per gallon.

Formula: Fuel Cost = (Miles / MPG) × Diesel Price

Average monthly cost: $6,241.97

Maximum monthly cost: $9,590.00

Minimum monthly cost: $3,981.67

View Graph 2 (PDF)

Conclusion

Diesel prices increased significantly in 2022 and declined afterwards. Higher diesel prices increase estimated trucking fuel costs. Fuel price monitoring is therefore important for transportation businesses.

These costs are estimates based on fixed mileage and fuel efficiency, not actual company expenses.
