The Impact of Diesel Prices on Trucking Fuel Costs in the United States

2020–2025 | Python Data Analysis

Research Question

How do changes in diesel prices affect trucking fuel costs in the United States?

Motivation

I am interested in trucking, finance and business management. Understanding fuel costs is important for transportation companies, financial planning and business decision-making.

Project Objectives

  • Analyze historical diesel prices.
  • Apply mathematical and statistical methods.
  • Estimate monthly trucking fuel costs.
  • Create data visualizations using Python.
  • Evaluate the financial impact of diesel price changes.

Data Source and Methodology

Source: U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) via FRED.

Period: 2020–2025

Observations: 72 months

Unit: US dollars per gallon

The data was imported, filtered, cleaned and analyzed using Python and Pandas. Visualizations were created using Matplotlib.

Business Implications

Understanding diesel price fluctuations can help transportation companies improve financial planning, fuel budgeting, operating cost management and freight pricing decisions.

Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates that higher diesel prices directly increase estimated trucking fuel costs when mileage and fuel efficiency remain constant.

Monitoring fuel prices is important for transportation businesses and financial decision-making.

Limitations

The calculated fuel costs are estimates based on fixed mileage and fuel efficiency. They do not represent actual company expenses.
